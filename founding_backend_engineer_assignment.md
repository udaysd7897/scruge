# The Assignment — Founding Backend Engineer

## Find the same product on other platforms

Give your program an Amazon product link. It should find the same product on the other platforms and tell you what it found on each one.

## What to match

The brand and model must match, plus:

| Category | Search on | Must also match |
|---|---|---|
| Smartphones | Flipkart, Croma | RAM and storage |
| Televisions | Flipkart, Croma | Screen size |
| Sneakers | Flipkart, Myntra | Colour |
| T-shirts, polos and jerseys | Flipkart, Myntra | Colour |

## What to show

**Input:** One Amazon product link from any of the four categories.

**Output:** For each platform, one of three results, each shown differently:

### In stock
Found the same product, and you can buy it. Show the link.

### Out of stock
Found the same product, but it cannot be bought right now. Show the link.

### Not listed
The platform does not sell this product at all.

## What to submit

1. **A simple document:** Explain how your matching works, as if you were explaining it to a 5-year-old.
2. **A vibe-coded website or app:** Paste an Amazon product link and see the results for the other platforms.

## Rules

You are free to use AI.

## The process

1. This assignment
2. An interview on problem-solving and culture fit
3. Two technical interviews
